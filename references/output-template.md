# Output Template

Use this structure for the final deliverable. Keep it under 1,500 words.

---

# [Product Name] UX Sentiment Research

**Purpose:** Pull what real users say about using the product, identify pattern-level UX friction, turn it into talking points and questions for the [role] interview.

**Sources scanned:** [list sources with rough volume, e.g., "Capterra (75+ reviews), G2 (34 reviews)"]

**Time spent:** ~[N] minutes

---

## The pattern: [one-sentence story of the overall sentiment]

[One short paragraph explaining the overall sentiment shape. Where do users light up? Where do they get stuck? What's the dominant emotion?]

Then a numbered list of the top 5 to 8 friction patterns:

1. **[Pattern name].** [One sentence describing the pattern, with at least one verbatim quote under 15 words attributed to a named source.]
2. **[Pattern name].** [Same shape.]
...

## What this tells you about the product team

[Two to four bullets reading the patterns as signals about how the team operates. Examples: "They ship a lot, IA hasn't caught up." "They lean on human support to plaster over discovery problems." "They have a clear value prop that users feel but the wide feature surface isn't navigable."]

---

## Two or three concrete improvements to bring up in the interview

Each improvement follows this shape:

### N. [Short title that frames the change, not the complaint]

[One sentence stating the problem with the quote attached. One sentence on why the team probably hasn't fixed it yet. Two or three sentences on the proposed change. One sentence on why it's worth doing now.]

Avoid generic improvements. Avoid "they should redesign the whole product." Bias toward things you could prototype in a week.

---

## Three to five sharp questions for the interview

Pick from these in the conversation. Don't run through all of them.

1. "[Specific finding from research]. Has the team explored [specific direction]? What's blocked that?"
2. "[Strategic question that reveals product judgment.]"
3. "[Question about process, research loop, or team operations grounded in a specific signal.]"
4. "[Question about a tradeoff the team is clearly making.]"

---

## What to verify in conversation

Three to five things you couldn't fully answer from external research. These show you did the work without pretending you have the whole picture.

---

## Honest read on the product's maturity stage

One short paragraph naming what stage the product is in. Common stages:
- **Pre-PMF**: still figuring out the value prop
- **Shipping fast, IA debt**: lots of features, navigation hasn't caught up
- **Mature, polishing**: core flows are settled, the work is refinement
- **Re-architecture window**: known patterns have hit ceilings, structural change coming
- **Late-stage maintenance**: feature surface frozen, team focused on retention

Name the stage. Say what that means for the work the role is hired to do.

---
